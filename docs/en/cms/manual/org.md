# Organizations

Every Kooboo account has its own organization; the account owner is the organization administrator. Admins can add members. After a member switches into an organization, they can manage sites they have permission for in that organization.

## Add a member

Two ways in:

- Click your username at the top right, then the second item in the menu (the current organization name).
- On the account home page, open **Account settings → Organization → More**.

Enter the person’s Kooboo **username** to add them to this organization.

## Switch organization

On the account home page, open **Account settings → Organization**, check the organization to enter, then click **Switch organization**. You are signed out; after you sign in again you are in that organization and can work on sites you have access to there, or create sites under it.

## Add a member as a site user

After someone joins the organization, they usually still need a site-level role before they can open a given site.

Open **Site settings → Site Users**, click **Add user**, then choose the user and role. They get the site permissions of that role.
